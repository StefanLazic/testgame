🐱 Claw Defense

Seven cats. Ten waves. One bowl of milk to protect.

Built for phones — tap a cat, tap the floor, watch the mice regret everything.

How to play

  • Place cats: tap a cat in the shop, then tap any free floor tile.
  • Earn fish 🐟: from every pest you stop and a bonus after each wave. Start a wave early for extra fish.
  • Upgrade: tap a placed cat to upgrade or sell it (sell refunds 70%).
  • Protect the milk 🥛: every pest that reaches it costs a life. Nine lives, no more.
  • Watch the sky: 🐦 birds fly straight over your maze — only some cats can hit them.
  • Curses: 🧙 the Witch hexes one pest a minute — frog, then stone, then dust. Bosses shrug it off.
  • Her Majesty: 👑 Mimi-chan costs a fortune, but every 10 s the whole board stops to bow.
  • Wave 5 & 10: a mini-boss and then something much, much worse.
